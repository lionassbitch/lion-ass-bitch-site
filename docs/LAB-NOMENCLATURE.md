## THE NAME

*What it is. Why it is. How it arose.*

Lion Ass Bitch.

Three words. Three animals. Three ranks.

Say it out loud and notice where your voice lands: on LION. The other two
words push it forward. Read the way it's built — not the way it sounds in a
hallway — it's a title, not an insult.

It's also a system. Lion moves. Ass holds. Bitch guards. Most people get one
of the three and call it a personality. Lion Ass Bitch is what it looks like
when you run all three at once.

### LION — SOVEREIGN WILL

The one who moves through the world unapologetically. Moves first, asks
questions later. Doesn't ask permission to take up space, because permission
was never on the table. In our world, that's **PRYDE**.

### ASS — STUBBORN TRUTH

The donkey. It carried every civilization you've ever heard of, and it gets
called stubborn for one reason: it will not walk into what it hasn't checked.
That isn't stupidity. It's loyalty to your own footing. Unwavering resilience —
the cosmic stubbornness that outlasts everyone who bet against you. Champions
are built out of it. In our world, that's **KICKZ**.

### BITCH — SACRED GUARDIAN

Strip the slur off and read the word. A bitch is a female canine: the one who
guards the den, and the one you don't cross while she's protecting what's hers.
Egypt put a jackal on that watch. Judgment. Boundaries. Transformation. In our
world, that's **ANPUT**.

### WHY IT IS

Because the words people use to shrink you are raw material.

We didn't clean the words up. We restored them. "Ass" and "bitch" were animals
before they were insults — a bearer and a guardian — and an insult only works
if you accept the speaker's definition. We use the dictionary's.

Put the lion in front and the whole phrase flips. That's the reclamation: the
exact sentence somebody spits at you becomes the rank you carry.

The initials spell LAB. A laboratory. Where things get tested, broken, and
rebuilt on purpose.

Lion Ass Bitch isn't a brand. It's a warning — to the world, that you're
coming, and to yourself, that you're done lying.

### HOW IT AROSE

It started as an insult aimed at somebody else.

A late-night Uber. A driver who wasn't where he said he'd be. An expanded state
of mind in the back seat. Somebody called him a lying ass bitch — and the words
landed as something else: Lion Ass Bitch.

One syllable of difference. A whole life inside it.

A lying ass bitch won't show up and won't own it. A Lion Ass Bitch is what you
become the second you stop lying — to yourself first.

It was a joke. Then a sketch. Then a company. Then the company became the thing
keeping its founder above water. Then it grew three faces and a job: do for the
next person what it did for him.

> "I turned a late Uber trip into a company, the company into a life jacket,
> and the life jacket into a myth."
> — Eddie Colón, Founder

Be the lion, not the liar.

## EXSUVERA PRESENTS

Exsuvera isn't a word you'll find anywhere else. It was coined — built piece
by piece, on purpose, by founder Eddie Colón — so the name would carry its own
instructions.

Take it apart.

| Part | Reading |
|---|---|
| **EX** | Latin. Out of. The way out. |
| **SU** | From Latin *sui*, of the self. In Spanish, simply: yours. |
| **VERA** | Latin *vera*, true. Spanish *de veras*: for real. |

Ex-su-vera. Out of your truth. For real.

There's a fourth word hiding in the middle: *exuviae* — the shell a creature
sheds once it has outgrown it. Snakes leave one. Cicadas leave one. The animal
doesn't die in the molt. The shape that stopped fitting gets left behind, and
the living thing walks out. Most people never get there. They mistake the shell
for the animal.

Read the whole word in one breath:

**Out of truth comes your true self.**

Not self-improvement — you were never broken. Self-archaeology. Strip the
layers that were never yours and find what was always there.

Exsuvera is the molt. Lion Ass Bitch is what walked out of it.

`[ ENTER LIONASSBITCH.COM ]`

**Locked reading direction:** "Out of truth comes your true self." Canon on
file previously read "from within the self, truth." Same loop, one line on the
page. Two reads like hedging.

**The molt, not the shell.** Exsuvera is the shedding, not the thing shed.
Never write "Exsuvera is the shell."

**Why the coinage is strong:** it reads in three languages at once — Latin,
Spanish, and the block — and all three land on the same word. A name that
instructs.

### Build spec

1. **Sticky syllables.** Beats 3, 4 and 5 are sticky: EX stays pinned on
   screen when SU arrives, SU stays when VERA arrives, and at beat 6 the three
   close up into one word. Everything else is a plain fade-up as it enters
   view. That single sticky move is the whole effect; the rest is restraint.
2. **Don't hijack the scroll.** Native scrolling with fade-on-entry only. The
   second scroll speed is controlled, mobile users bail — and this page's
   entire job is delivering the click to LionAssBitch.com. A reveal that costs
   the conversion it was built to earn is decoration.
3. **Sticky ENTER** in the corner from beat 2 onward, so anyone already sold
   can leave early.
4. **Text in the page at load**, at zero opacity. If the copy is injected by
   JavaScript on scroll, search engines see a blank gate and the origin story
   indexes as nothing.

Implemented in this repo: `app/components/WordReveal.tsx` (beats, sticky
stack, sticky exit CTA) over `app/components/ScrollReveal.tsx`
(IntersectionObserver fade-on-entry, flags `data-js` on mount so copy is only
hidden when JS can reveal it), and the `wordReveal` / `revealBeat` rules in
`app/system.css`.

## PAGE ORDER (ExsuveraPresents.com)

   should never have to read the explainer.

## OPEN FLAGS

- **Canon fork — Sacred Guardian vs. Sacred Reckoning.** The thread titles
  the third rank SACRED GUARDIAN and names ANPUT. `docs/LAB-CANON.md`, the
  dossiers, and the atelier skill still say KHEMETZ / Sacred Reckoning. The
  nomenclature copy keeps GUARDIAN in the title and judgment / boundaries in the
  body so both survive, and links to the Khemetz dossier. Pick one across the
  trinity grid and dossier sheets, or the myth forks at the front door.
- **The psychedelic dial.** "An expanded state of mind" is true, on-brand for
  Exsuvera, and survives a partner or investor landing on it. Say the word to
  make it explicit.
- **Ownability.** A coined word with no prior meaning is the strongest
  trademark class there is (fanciful), and a public page documenting who built
  it, from what, and why is first-use evidence. If EXSUVERA isn't filed yet,
  this copy going live is the moment to do it. Not legal advice; just the
  sequence.
